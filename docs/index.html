How it works

Set it up once. Forget it's there.

Clipline starts with your computer and waits in the tray. You only notice it when you need it.

01

Choose

Pick your clips folder, clip length, quality and your key. Clipline shows the RAM it needs before you start.

02

Play

Game, stream, work – Clipline keeps the last minutes in memory, using your graphics card so nothing slows down.

03

Clip

Something great happened? Press your key. The last moments land as an MP4 in your folder, with sound.

FAQ

Questions

Does it record all the time?

Yes, but only into memory. Nothing is written to your disk until you press your key, and old footage is thrown away continuously.

Will it slow down my games?

Clipline encodes on your graphics card, so the CPU cost is tiny. If no hardware encoder is available it falls back to a very fast software encoder.

How much RAM does it need?

It depends on clip length and quality – the setup shows the estimate live. A 1-minute clip at 1080p60 needs roughly 230 MB in total.

Is it really free?

Yes. Clipline is open source under the MIT license.
